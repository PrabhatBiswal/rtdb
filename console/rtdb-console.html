Sign in to browse the shard. Read-only.

Sessions last one hour. Access is revocable per user (§10).

Users

A viewer reads. An editor and an owner can also set, add and delete nodes — enforced by the GATEWAY on the token's role, not by this page. Passwords are stored hashed and can never be shown again — a forgotten one is reset, not looked up. Removing a user, or resetting their password, does not end a session already open — that is kick.ts (§10).
